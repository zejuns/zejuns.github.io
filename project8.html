NFT Marketplace: voxeltide.com

These are my undergraduate capstone projects for two majors, each with a distinct focus. The design aspect may differ from the final website. On the technical side, the front end is developed using React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS for the user interface, while the back end leverages the Ethereum blockchain and smart contracts for decentralized functionality.

Thanks to Daulat Hussain's for introducing me to Web3 development.

Empowering 3D Creators with Blockchain and NFTs

This project aims to create a seamless bridge between 3D designers, consumers, and 3D printing services through blockchain-based NFTs. Traditional methods of protecting and monetizing digital designs can be complex and limiting, especially for independent creators. By leveraging NFTs, we offer a transparent and secure way to verify ownership, facilitate transactions, and enable new revenue opportunities. Whether selling digital models, licensing designs, or turning them into physical products, this platform provides a dynamic ecosystem for creators to thrive.

Product Design Preview

Left: 3D Printing Module (with Control System)
Uses FFF (Fused Filament Fabrication) printhead with precise X/Y/Z motion. Horizontal conveyor transports finished prints to the storage side.

Right: Smart Pickup & Storage System
Multiple independent shelves, each receiving printed items from the left conveyor. Combining rapid pickup with categorized storage for user self-service. A touchscreen provides model selection, status monitoring, and control.
