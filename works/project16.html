AI-Powered Integrated Stove

This compact kitchen appliance combines a gas burner and an induction cooktop with an overhead camera for visual feedback. The induction cooktop has a built-in scale for precise ingredient measurement, while the countertop displays real-time cooking guidance. Angled surfaces replace sharp corners for a safe, intuitive control hub. A lighting-guided seasoning module signals when to add spices and dispenses the right amount automatically.
