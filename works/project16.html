AI-Powered Integrated Stove

This compact hybrid cooktop integrates gas and induction heating, with an overhead camera enabling computer vision. The induction zone on the right incorporates a built-in scale for precision weighing. A flat surface functions as an integrated display for real-time visual guidance, while the angled interface serves as the central control hub, enhancing ergonomics and operational safety. A light-guided module automatically dispenses precise spice measurements.

Developed in collaboration with Midea, a Fortune Global 500 company.
