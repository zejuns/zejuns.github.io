Yunxian Brand Design

In the Product Derivative Design course, under the guidance of Professor Yang Jing, we undertook a branding project for Xiao Mangtu Village, located in Yun County, Lincang City, Yunnan Province. As part of this project, we created a village brand identity and designed packaging for a local rice noodle brand.

Teamwork
I was primarily responsible for preliminary research, rendering, and scene generation.
