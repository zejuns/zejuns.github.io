About

Zejun Huang combines arts and technology, excelling in modeling, rendering, and animation. He has won design awards such as the EPDA Winner, G-CROSS Bronze Award, and was shortlisted for the Red Dot Design Award, along with holding multiple Chinese patents. With a dual degree in Computer Science and Technology, he is skilled in programming. The MAT program at UCSB aligns perfectly with his diverse background.
